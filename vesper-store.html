Light is a material

We buy the colour of the light before we buy the lamp.

Warm doesn't mean orange. Below about 2200 K a room turns amber and every wood in it goes muddy; above 3000 K skin starts to read grey and the evening feels like an office. The window sits between.

Every VESPER light is specified at 2400 K, CRI 95+ and dims to a candle without shifting hue. Drag the scale — the whole page is lit by it.
