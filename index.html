Why Lumira

Built for the hour the day exhales.

Lumira began with a small observation: the big light is always wrong after eight. Most rooms are lived in by lamplight but lit for cleaning. So we make small sculptural lights that do what ceiling bulbs can't — lower the brightness, add colour, give the evening a shape.

The collection stays deliberately small. Eight pieces, chosen slowly, tested in real bedrooms and rented flats, kept only if we'd cross a city to buy them again. No seasonal churn, no endless catalogue — when something better arrives, it replaces a piece rather than joining it.

Before you order

What to expect.

Shipping

Dispatched within 48 hours from our EU warehouse, tracked door to door. Most orders arrive in 3–6 business days — no customs, no surprises inside the Union.

Power

Every light runs on a standard EU plug or USB-C, at low voltage. Set it down, plug it in — there is nothing to install, mount, or configure.

Care

Matte shells prefer a soft, dry cloth. Mist-lamps like clean water and an occasional rinse. Everything else just likes being switched on.

Good to know

Questions, answered quietly.

How long will my order take to arrive?

We dispatch within 48 hours from our warehouse in the EU. Delivery typically takes 3–6 business days depending on your country, and every parcel travels tracked, door to door.

What if it doesn't suit my space?

Return it within 30 days in its original box for a full refund. No forms to hunt down — one email, and we send you a label. We'd rather a light live somewhere it's loved.

What about plugs and power?

Everything in the collection runs on a standard EU two-pin plug or USB-C, at low voltage. A light should never need an electrician — plug it in and it works.

Is there a warranty?

Every piece carries the full two-year EU warranty. If a light fails through normal use within that time, we repair or replace it — your choice.

Can I send one as a gift?

Yes — every piece already ships in a matte, gift-ready box with no prices inside. At checkout you can add an optional gift note, handwritten by us with an actual pen.
