Approach

Treatment begins with a plan, not with an implant. Every case is run as a complete reconstruction plan — from diagnostics and surgery, through prosthetics, to occlusal function — with the hardest situations handled alongside a team of specialists from several disciplines.

Digital smile design lets the patient see the planned result before treatment begins. Since qualifying at the Silesian Medical Academy in 2003, that is more than twenty years of practice devoted to a single thing — predictability of the outcome.
